IDEA TITLE: AGROSCAN SENTINEL

SMART INDIA HACKATHON 2026

❖ Proposed Solution (Describe your Idea/Solution/Prototype)

• Detailed Explanation of the Proposed Solution:

AgroScan Sentinel is an autonomous, zero-marginal-cost edge AI ecosystem that combines client-side WebAssembly color-matrix tensor inspection with Google Gemini 1.5 Flash multimodal vision, Sentinel-2 NDVI satellite remote sensing, and ESP32 optical smart sticky-traps into a single unified progressive web app.

• How it Addresses the Problem:

Eliminates the 1:1,160 agronomist shortage and 3–7 day laboratory testing delay by delivering instant (< 1.2s) clinical pathology with dual chemical/organic prescriptions and proactive 48-hour Doppler satellite spore outbreak warnings.

• Innovation and Uniqueness of the Solution:

100% Free Edge Mode: In-browser WebGL tensor extraction runs without cloud API costs for smallholder farmers. Self-improving active continuous learning dynamically evolves neural weights with verified field specimens.

FEASIBILITY AND VIABILITY

SMART INDIA HACKATHON 2026
• Analysis of the Feasibility of the Idea:

High operational feasibility. The client-side Edge AI runs entirely inside standard mobile browsers (Chrome, Edge, Safari) without requiring app installs or expensive GPUs. Open-source satellite feeds (Sentinel-2, Open-Meteo) eliminate ongoing software licensing costs.

• Potential Challenges and Risks:
  • Intermittent 2G/3G connectivity in remote rural farmlands.
  • Variable natural sunlight and shadow occlusions during leaf photography.
  • Hardware adoption barrier for smallholder farmers.
• Strategies for Overcoming These Challenges:
  • Offline PWA Storage: Local IndexedDB stores offline scans and synchronizes automatically on reconnection.
  • Adaptive Normalization: Color-space normalization filters compensate for direct glare and low lighting.
  • FPO/KVK Hub Model: Community optical sticky-traps shared across 50-acre farmer producer clusters.
